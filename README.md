### Where Every Detail Matters

Juanita's Boutique Cleaning brings a couture approach to home care. This is not a franchise operation - it's a carefully curated cleaning experience designed for those who appreciate the art of a truly immaculate home. The website reflects this philosophy with elegant design, meticulous organization, and an attention to detail that mirrors the service itself.

Every surface, every corner, every detail is treated with the care of a conservator restoring fine art. The website communicates this elevated standard through:

- **Refined Typography**: Cormorant Garamond for elegance, clean sans-serif for clarity

## The Product Philosophy

Every product used in a Juanita cleaning meets the **Five Pillars Standard**:

1. **Effective**: Must outperform conventional alternatives
2. **Non-Toxic**: Safe for children, pets, and sensitive individuals
3. **Sustainable**: Packaging and sourcing with conscience
4. **Beautiful**: Aesthetic presentation matters
5. **Traceable**: Every ingredient origin known and transparent
